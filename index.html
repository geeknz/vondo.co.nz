Site Proof

Site records, in seconds.

NZ construction subbies write up site records at the end of the day from memory — or not at all. When head contractors ask for the paper trail, it doesn't exist.

Site Proof fixes that. Snap a photo, tap the mic, and describe what you see. Site Proof writes the record. Review it, sign it, share it as a PDF.

How it works

Three steps. Under a minute. No typing.

Tap the mic and describe what you see. No typing with dirty hands.

Send it

Site Proof writes the record. Review, sign, and share as a PDF to the head contractor.
